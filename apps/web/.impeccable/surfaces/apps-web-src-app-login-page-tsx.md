## Scope and mode
Public authentication page at `apps/web/src/app/login/page.tsx`. Operate.

## Audience, task, and action
Returning Roleway users need to enter their email and password, recover clear validation feedback, preserve a safe requested destination, and continue to their workspace. The only primary action is Log in; account creation links to the separate signup route.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
Minimal centered authentication in the structural spirit of Linear and Vercel: one narrow form on the Roleway paper field, a precise header with the route mark, and no side panel, card, illustration, or marketing copy.

## Constraints
Keep labels visible, keyboard focus explicit, mobile targets at least 40px, errors actionable, and the signup link easy to find without competing with the form.
