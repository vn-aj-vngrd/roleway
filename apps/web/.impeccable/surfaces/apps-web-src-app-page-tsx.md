## Scope and mode
Public landing page at `apps/web/src/app/page.tsx`. Persuade.

## Audience, job, action, and proof
Individual job seekers running one or several deliberate career searches should understand that Roleway separates each strategy, then keeps every serious Opportunity, its working context, and one concrete next action together. Primary action: start or open Roleway. Proof comes from real HTML representations of the Search Project switcher, Opportunity list/board, Job Inbox, application context, interview preparation, Today queue, and optional Assist; no fabricated metrics, testimonials, or outcomes.

## Chosen direction and memorable moment
Architectural working pin-up, selected from seed `165e5825`, paced as a Linear-style product narrative without copying Linear’s identity. The first viewport is remembered for the thesis “Run every career search like a project,” one edge-to-edge project-scoped Opportunities workspace, and a Waypoint Blue next-action slip. The scroll moves through fragmentation, separate search strategy, the review-to-decision loop, connected Opportunity context, and human-controlled Assist using real HTML product scenes.

## Constraints
Keep the page product-led, premium, and minimal despite the deeper story. Waypoint Blue is the only saturated voice and carries primary actions, focus, selection, progress, and the route mark. Product demonstrations use inert visual controls rather than fake buttons. Keep all body/utility contrast at WCAG AA. Avoid generic icon-card grids, AI-first framing, fake screenshots, fabricated outcomes, and claims that Roleway applies or contacts employers.
