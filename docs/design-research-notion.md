# Notion workspace design research for Roleway

Researched from Notion’s official product, help, and release material on 21 August 2026. Roleway should adopt the current workspace grammar—not Notion’s identity, collaboration model, or AI-first positioning.

## Current system principles

1. **Navigation is a workspace, not a dashboard menu.** Notion’s sidebar combines workspace switching, search, Home, Inbox, Library, settings, and nested content in one persistent rail. The current redesign organizes this into top-level tabs and user-controlled sections to prevent crowding. Roleway should keep a persistent, calm rail and group destinations by the job-search workflow rather than exposing a feature inventory. [Notion Help, “Navigate Notion with the sidebar”](https://www.notion.com/help/navigate-with-the-sidebar) · [Notion 3.4 release](https://www.notion.com/releases/2026-03-26)
2. **Selection is quiet and structural.** Notion’s current sidebar uses soft neutral fills, restrained graphite text, consistent line icons, and rounded row selection rather than saturated navigation chrome. Roleway should reserve blue for actions and progress while using warm-neutral selection surfaces for navigation.
3. **The interface is configurable without becoming visually busy.** Notion lets users collapse, reorder, hide, and resize sidebar sections. Roleway’s compact sidebar and grouped Workspace/Tools structure should remain, but its geometry and states should feel like one adaptable rail instead of separate containers.
4. **Content owns the canvas.** Notion pages use broad white reading surfaces, generous internal margins, strong titles, subdued metadata, and minimal outer chrome. Roleway’s main content island should retain its boundary while becoming visually quieter and more page-like inside.
5. **Controls stay close to what they affect.** Search, creation, filters, and view actions are placed in the region they modify. Roleway should avoid dashboard-style detached toolbars and keep actions with page titles, lists, and pipeline stages.
6. **Density is calm, not sparse.** Current Notion navigation uses compact rows and labels while preserving enough whitespace to scan sections. Linear contributes interaction density and keyboard efficiency; Apple contributes legibility, alignment, and finish. Roleway should combine those qualities without cloning any product’s branding.

## Applied direction

- Warm stone application backdrop and sidebar, paper-white main island.
- Graphite text with quiet gray metadata; Roleway blue only for primary actions, progress, focus, and selected icons.
- Flat navigation rail with subtle neutral selection, 32–36px rows, and consistent 16px icons.
- Main pages read as documents and workspaces, not nested dashboard cards.
- Hairline separators and soft local fills replace strong borders and blue-tinted chrome.
- Compact and expanded sidebar modes preserve the same centerline and interaction model.
- Dark mode remains available through the same semantic roles.
