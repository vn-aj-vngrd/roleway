## Findings

### Let the work own the attention

Linear’s current interface principle is explicit: navigation and orientation should recede while the user’s task stays in focus. Its refresh reduces icon treatments, softens structural lines, and uses hierarchy rather than equal visual weight everywhere. For Roleway’s landing page, the product interface therefore needs to be the visual evidence—not a decorative frame around it.

### Rhythm is a system, not repeated spacing

Notion’s page refresh treats adjacent content as a system. Lists compress while prose gets more breathing room, creating predictable flow without forcing every block into the same spacing. The Roleway page now varies its pace: a dense split hero, a large product proof, a dark statement section, an interactive workspace, and slower workflow chapters.

### Use simple material with a recognizable mechanism

Notion’s brand work translates its block-based product mechanism into modular identity rather than applying unrelated decoration. Roleway’s corresponding mechanism is the flush working plane: sidebar, separator, route toolbar, and opportunity content. Marketing previews now use that same structure throughout.

### Screenshots should communicate benefits, not merely existence

Apple recommends using real UI screenshots, making the first images communicate the app’s essence, and focusing each subsequent image on a primary benefit or feature. Roleway therefore shows a real pipeline first, then lets visitors inspect eight actual working views, followed by three focused workflow scenes.

### Simplicity is not emptiness

Apple defines simplicity as removing friction while preserving enough context to make the interface understandable. Strong hierarchy, concise language, alignment, and grouping make a sparse composition useful; empty whitespace alone does not. The revised landing uses asymmetry, scale, tonal contrast, and real content to make quiet space intentional.

### Motion must preserve agency

Apple’s guidance emphasizes agency and reduced-motion support; Linear describes interaction details as the sum of useful feedback rather than spectacle. Roleway keeps bounded scroll reveals and removes the decorative route-progress ornament. Motion supports reading order and product entry, and becomes effectively static when reduced motion is requested.

## Applied direction

- Asymmetric hero: product thesis on the left, precise explanation and action on the right.
- The actual application shell is the recurring visual signature.
- One graphite section creates a deliberate peak in an otherwise light-first page.
- Product previews use the new flush sidebar/content design with one separator—no application island.
- Feature exploration remains interactive and shows all eight implemented workspaces.
- Workflow chapters focus on one decision at a time and use real HTML UI rather than generic illustrations.
- No gradients, glass cards, fake customer claims, invented metrics, robot imagery, sparkles, or AI-first positioning.
