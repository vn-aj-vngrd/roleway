# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individual job seekers running an active, multi-month search. They may be employed, between roles, or changing careers; they need one dependable workspace for deciding what matters and moving each serious application forward.

## Product Purpose

Roleway turns a job hunt into a controlled project. It connects discovery, evaluation, application work, follow-up, interviews, and outcomes around a durable Opportunity so the user can always answer “what should I do next?” Success means fewer dropped follow-ups, stronger evidence-based applications, and clear ownership of every external action.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, and exact. The voice is direct and useful rather than motivational or promotional. The interface should create confidence through visible context, provenance, and progress.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic admin dashboard, Trello clone, CRM template, mass auto-apply bot, resume-generator funnel, or AI-chat wrapper. Avoid startup gradients, glass surfaces, glowing AI controls, oversized cards, decorative metrics, robots, sparkles, and default shadcn composition. Use Linear, GitHub, Raycast, and Arc only as references for speed, density, and interaction discipline—not as visual templates.

## Design Principles

1. **The next action leads.** Active work always resolves to a clear, concrete next step.
2. **Workflow over feature inventory.** Tools appear where the opportunity needs them, not as disconnected destinations.
3. **Evidence over scores.** Recommendations expose requirements, sources, gaps, and approved career evidence.
4. **Prepare, then ask.** AI drafts internal work and shows its execution; users approve consequential changes and own all external actions.
5. **Dense, never noisy.** Preserve useful context with typography, alignment, and progressive disclosure rather than card grids.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All primary flows work with keyboard and pointer, focus is visible, drag-and-drop has button and keyboard alternatives, status never depends on color alone, dialogs manage focus, and motion respects reduced-motion preferences. Mobile preserves Today, status changes, notes, tasks, and preparation workflows.
