## Priority issues

1. **P1 — Product preview is too miniature.** At marketing scale, interface text becomes noise and resembles a wireframe.
2. **P1 — The page loses momentum after the first reveal.** Large vertical intervals and repeated pale sections create emotional valleys.
3. **P1 — Composition is category-familiar.** The centered SaaS hero does not make the job-search journey the dominant grammar.
4. **P2 — Typography has hierarchy but little character.** Large headings and tiny UI labels create an abrupt scale gap.
5. **P2 — Landing and product use separate density systems.** Editorial whitespace and compressed app UI do not feel continuous.
